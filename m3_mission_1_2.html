Expressions: Invitations and Responses

Study & Understand: ฟังและฝึกพูดตามตาราง (Track 1-08)

✅ Accept (การตอบรับคำชวน)

  • Of course! I'd love to! (แน่นอน! ฉันอยากไป)
  • I'd love to! Thanks for asking.
  • Sounds great. / With pleasure!
  • Sure! That sounds like fun.

❌ Decline (การปฏิเสธอย่างสุภาพ)

  • Sorry, I can't. I have to study. (ขอโทษที ไปไม่ได้ ฉันต้องอ่านหนังสือ)
  • Thanks for the offer, but I have to refuse.
  • Sorry, I'd love to, but I don't have the energy to do aerobics now.
  • I'd love to, but I have another commitment. (อยากไปนะ แต่ติดธุระอื่นแล้ว)

Reading & Comprehension

Different Interests

⛸️ Carlos

I love ice skating. At the moment, I'm taking private lessons, and I'm applying to a special skating school. They train many future champions there. I hope I get in! I have two dreams. One of my dreams is to get a gold medal in ice skating at the Olympics. My other dream is to become an ice-skating coach. I want to help younger skaters become champions, too.

🏹 Sarah

I do a lot of different things. I'm on my school's softball team, and I swim with my friends on the weekend. But now I have a favorite hobby: archery! Yes, really! Archery is a great sport because it's more about form and technique than strength. Still, I do a lot of pushups to strengthen my arms. Competitions are a thrill, but it's satisfying just to compete against myself. When my arrows hit the center of the target, I can be really proud.

🗣️ Nicholas

This may sound strange, but I really love studying languages. We move to other countries pretty often because of my dad's job, so I hear a lot of different languages. Some are very similar to each other, like Spanish and Portuguese. Other languages are completely different. I practice translating from one language to another. Someday I want to have a career as an interpreter.

🎸 Carmen

My interests are all related to music. I love all kinds, from hip-hop to opera and from rock to folk. I'm learning songs in English, Spanish, and French. I sing in our school chorus and have a lot of solos. On weekends, I play guitar with a band. When I'm making music, I forget everything else. I'm hoping to have a great career in music. In my dreams, I'm performing in concerts around the world.
