🎧 Track 2-08 (Fashion Trend of the Past)
📜 Fashion Trend of the Past
Paragraph 1 _______________________

In ancient Egypt, both men and women shaved their heads and wore decorated wigs. Wealthy or royal people wore heavy collars made of gold and jewels. They wore perfume, makeup, and fine clothes to show that they were important. But their fashions were also practical. Shaving people's heads prevented lice. The heavy black makeup around their eyes protected them from the sun. Creamy perfumes helped keep their skin from drying out and were even used to brush their teeth.

Paragraph 2 _______________________

High heels are popular with women today. But high heels were first popular among men! In the fifteenth century, high heels were worn by Persian soldiers. The heels helped keep their feet in place when they rode horses. Then European men copied the style. They wore high heels to show how rich and powerful they were. Women wore high heels to show how important their husbands were. Some heels were so high that people needed servants to walk beside them so they wouldn't fall. It is said that King Louis XIV, a seventeenth-century French king, wore heels that were almost ten centimeters tall. Clearly, those high heels were not for riding horses!

Paragraph 3 _______________________

In the sixteenth century, most people had healthy teeth. This is because sugar was not in most food, since it was very expensive. One person who loved sweets and had enough money to buy them was Queen Elizabeth I of England. She loved sweets so much that some people say her teeth were black. But instead of seeing black teeth as dirty, people saw them as trendy. People started to blacken their teeth so that they could look rich and noble like Queen Elizabeth.
